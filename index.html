14 Si

Silicon (Si → SiO₂, ×2.1393) — Lithium Borate Fusion / WD-XRF

Fused at 1000–1050 °C with Li2B4O7/LiBO2 flux (1:5 to 1:10 sample-to-flux) in Pt/Au crucibles into homogeneous glass disks for WD-XRF or dissolved in dilute HNO3/HCl for ICP-OES.

Benchmark Elements:

1. Lithium Borate Fusion (Li₂B₄O₇ / LiBO₂)

  • Target Suite: Si, Al, Fe, Ca, Mg, Na, K, Ti, Mn, P, Sr, Ba (+ Y & REE)
  • Conditions: 1000–1050 °C in 95%Pt–5%Au crucibles (1:5 to 1:10 flux ratio)
  • Sulfide Rule: Pre-roast sulfide-rich ores at 600–650 °C to prevent Pt/Au crucible attack
  • Finish: WD-XRF fused glass disk (with LOI) or HNO3/HCl digest for ICP-OES/ICP-MS

2. Sodium Peroxide Fusion (Na₂O₂)

  • Target Suite: Refractory Cr, Sn, W, Zr, Hf, Nb, Ta, Be, Mo & Boron (B)
  • Conditions: 480–650 °C oxidative sinter/fusion in Zr, Ni, or glassy-carbon crucibles
  • Crucible Rule: Avoid Zr crucibles for Zr/Hf assays; avoid Li-borate flux for Boron
  • Finish: Water/HCl leach of fusion cake followed by ICP-OES or ICP-MS

3. Stoichiometric Oxide Conversion Factors

  • Si / Al / Fe: Si→SiO2: ×2.1393 | Al→Al2O3: ×1.8895 | Fe→Fe2O3: ×1.4297
  • Ca / Mg: Ca→CaO: ×1.3992 | Mg→MgO: ×1.6583
  • Na / K: Na→Na2O: ×1.3480 | K→K2O: ×1.2046
  • Ti / P / Cr: Ti→TiO2: ×1.6681 | P→P2O5: ×2.2914 | Cr→Cr2O3: ×1.4616
